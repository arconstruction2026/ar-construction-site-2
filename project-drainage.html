Project Overview

This project delivered culvert and roadside drainage infrastructure for the local Pradeshiya Sabha, designed to manage consistent water flow and prevent flooding along the road corridor during heavy rainfall.

Works included culvert construction, drain channel lining, and outfall detailing — all executed to local government specification standards and coordinated with the surrounding civil works.

Careful attention was paid to flow capacity and long-term durability, ensuring the drainage system performs reliably across monsoon seasons with minimal maintenance.
