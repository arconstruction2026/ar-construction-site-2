Project Overview

This project involved the construction of a retaining wall spanning 25 meters in length and standing approximately 19 feet in height, designed to stabilize sloped terrain and prevent soil erosion in the area.

The wall was engineered to withstand lateral earth pressure while ensuring long-term structural stability. Execution followed strict quality and safety protocols, with careful attention to drainage integration and foundation strength to guarantee durability under varying load and weather conditions.

All work was carried out to Rural Development Department (RDD) specification standards, with full documentation maintained for government audit and records.
