Project Overview

This project involved the construction of a water turnout structure spanning 6 meters in length and standing 16 feet in height, designed to control and divert water from the river into agricultural irrigation channels serving nearby paddy fields.

The gate structure was engineered to regulate water flow efficiently, ensuring consistent water supply for cultivation while maintaining structural integrity against river currents and seasonal water level changes.

Execution followed Irrigation Department specification standards, with attention to gate mechanism durability and long-term water management reliability.
